## Full Description (max 4000 characters)
Immerse yourself in ZenNurikabe, a peaceful and deeply rewarding Japanese grid logic puzzle inspired by the classic Nikoli masterpiece "Nurikabe" (Islands in the Stream).

In Japanese folklore, Nurikabe is an invisible wall that misleads travelers. In this meditative puzzle, your objective is to carve out serene, numbered garden islands surrounded by a continuous flowing ocean stream.

### 🌊 How to Play
• **Numbered Islands**: Each island on the grid contains exactly one numbered clue, representing the exact number of squares comprising that island.
• **Island Isolation**: Islands cannot touch each other orthogonally (horizontally or vertically). They must be completely separated by stream water.
• **Continuous Stream**: All ocean stream squares must connect together to form one unbroken, flowing river across the whole grid.
• **No 2×2 Pools**: You cannot form a solid 2×2 block of ocean stream water anywhere.

### ✨ Zen Features
• **50 Handcrafted Levels**: 5 beautifully balanced chapters ranging from gentle 5×5 introductory gardens to challenging 9×9 stone archipelagos.
• **Pure Procedural Acoustics**: Soothing audio synthesized in real time via Web Audio API—crystal water droplet plucks, dry bamboo ticks, stream ripple harmonics, and resonant temple gongs. No external sound samples or downloads needed.
• **Smart Deduction Hints**: Get gentle guidance whenever you're stuck, highlighting forced moves, isolated water streams, or island borders.
• **Fluid Touch Controls**: Tap to cycle states or drag smoothly across cells to paint flowing stream channels. Full undo and level reset support.
• **100% Offline & Private**: Zero data collection, no internet connection required, no timers, and no banner advertisements. Play anytime, anywhere.

Find your flow with ZenNurikabe!
